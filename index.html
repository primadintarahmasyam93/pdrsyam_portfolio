About

About Me

I am an Environmental Scientist and Geospatial Researcher with over a decade of experience spanning remote sensing, GIS, and spatial planning across Indonesia. My work integrates Earth observation, Google Earth Engine–based geospatial analysis, and environmental modelling to study coastal ecosystem change, mangrove habitat health, and landscape ecology under climate change. I hold a Master of Science in Environmental Science from Universitas Gadjah Mada, graduating Cum Laude, and I bridge technical geospatial analysis with detailed spatial planning (RDTR) and disaster risk assessment to support climate-resilient, evidence-based policy for Indonesia's coastal regions.

Focus Areas

Research Pillars

The scientific foundations that define my interdisciplinary research — integrating Earth Observation, GeoAI, Environmental Modelling, and Landscape Ecology to develop Nature-based Solutions for climate-resilient coastal systems, embedded within spatial planning to realise spatial justice between humans and nature.

Earth Observation

Multitemporal monitoring with Landsat, SPOT, and SAR imagery for coastal and land-cover change detection.

GeoAI & Cloud Computing

Large-scale analysis pipelines in Google Earth Engine for mangrove health and habitat suitability mapping.

Environmental Modelling

Simulating landscape ecosystem change and climate adaptation scenarios with InVEST, Terrset, and DSAS.

Landscape Ecology

Assessing landscape structure, karst geomorphology, and disaster risk in vulnerable coastal terrain.

Coastal Ecosystems & Blue Carbon

Evaluating mangrove ecosystems for erosion mitigation, carbon storage, and ecosystem-based adaptation.

Spatial Planning (RDTR)

Leading detailed spatial plans and land-use assessments across Java, Papua, Aceh, and Kalimantan.

Coastal Multi-Hazard Assessment

Mapping chronic geomorphological degradation and multi-hazard exposure along Indonesia's coastlines.

Spatial Statistics

Applying Stata, Python, and SPSS for spatial statistical analysis and environmental data modelling — from parameter weighting to predictive ecosystem models.
